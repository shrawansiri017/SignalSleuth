Fake News Detection

  1. Open Fake News Detection.
  2. Paste a news article into the text box.
  3. Click Analyze News.
  4. View the prediction, confidence and contributing words.
🖼️

Image Detection

  1. Open Image Detection.
  2. Select an image from your device.
  3. Preview the selected image.
  4. Click Analyze Image to view the result.
🎬

Video Detection

  1. Open Video Detection.
  2. Upload a supported video file.
  3. Preview your selected video.
  4. Click Analyze Video to view the result.
COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Is SignalSleuth free to use?

Guests can perform up to four successful detections. Registered users can continue using the detection tools.

Which image formats are supported?

JPG, JPEG, PNG and WEBP image formats are supported.

Which video formats are supported?

MP4, MOV, AVI, MKV and WEBM formats are supported, subject to the upload size limit.

Are the AI predictions always accurate?

No. AI predictions are estimates based on model outputs. They should not be treated as definitive proof that content is real or fake.

Why is my image or video analysis failing?

Check that your file uses a supported format, is not corrupted and is within the 50 MB upload limit. Images containing clear human faces may be more suitable for deepfake analysis.

Can I view my previous detections?

Registered users can view their detection history from the Dashboard.
