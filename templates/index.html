ABOUT THE PROJECT

Making digital media analysis more understandable.

SignalSleuth is an academic mini-project exploring how machine learning can assist in identifying suspicious news patterns and potential deepfake media.

By combining text classification, image analysis, video analysis and explainability, the platform demonstrates multiple approaches to digital content analysis.
